Othello

Play against another player or the computer on a 6×6 or 8×8 board.

Quick demo

1. Pick a highlighted move.

2. Trap a white disc between two black discs.

3. Captured discs flip to your colour.

Rules

Players take turns placing black and white discs. A legal move must capture at least one opponent disc in a straight line.

Captured discs are flipped to the current player. The game ends when neither player has a legal move. The player with more discs wins.

Highlighted cells show legal moves for the current player.
